# ATS Checklist

## Format

- Prefer DOCX or text-based PDF.
- Use one column.
- Keep contact info in the document body.
- Avoid tables, text boxes, headers/footers for key content, images, icons, skill bars, and charts.
- Use common fonts such as Arial, Calibri, Helvetica, Georgia, or Times New Roman.
- Keep body text around 10-12 pt and margins at 0.5-1 inch.

## Sections

Use standard headings:

- Professional Summary
- Technical Skills or Skills
- Professional Experience or Work Experience
- Education
- Certifications
- Projects, if relevant

## Keyword Workflow

1. Extract hard skills, platforms, tools, methodologies, certifications, seniority signals, and domain terms from the target job.
2. Classify keywords:
   - Critical: required qualifications and repeated title-defining terms.
   - Important: preferred qualifications and recurring responsibility terms.
   - Nice-to-have: optional or low-frequency terms.
3. Map each keyword to evidence in the profile.
4. Add supported terms naturally to summary, skills, and bullets.
5. Leave unsupported terms out and report them as gaps.

## Final Check

- Critical supported keywords appear naturally.
- No keyword stuffing.
- Dates and section labels are consistent.
- Text is selectable and parseable.
- File name is clear, such as `FirstName_LastName_Resume_TargetRole.docx`.
